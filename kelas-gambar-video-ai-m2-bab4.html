Buat Poster Kota kelahiran dengan ChatGPT

1Buka Aplikasi ChatGPT
2Pilih Buat Gambar
3Masukan Foto yang ingin digunakan
4Copy dan Paste Prompt di Berikut dan ganti kata-kata yang ada di dalam kurung
PROMPT

Prompt

a portrait size wallpaper containing a subject whose photo is attached shows his/her birthplace from the city of (BANDUNG), the wallpaper displays visuals of what is in the subject's birthplace, the wallpaper style is cinematic, the subject is wearing a contemporary casual outfit with stylish motifs, colors, and types that do not look stiff and monotonous, the subject's expression should not be the same as the attached photo of the subject, the subject's expression looks cheerful and happy, all visuals presented in the wallpaper must be precise, above the subject there is the writing "BANDUNG DICIPTAKAN SAAT TUHAN TERSENYUM", Don't forget to include the city's typical slogan, every piece of writing on the wallpaper must be eye-catching, not monotonous and boring to the eye.8K resolution
CATATAN

Ganti kata yang ada di dalam kurung dengan kota yang diinginkan. Pada contoh prompt di atas, kota yang digunakan adalah BANDUNG.
